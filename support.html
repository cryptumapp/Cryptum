Common questions

How do Projects, Jobs and Tasks fit together?

A Project is a big area of your life or work, like "Home" or "Studio". Inside it, Jobs are pieces of work with an end, like "Kitchen renovation". A Job's Tasks are the steps that get it done. To see it in action, open Settings › Data & Backup › Add Sample Project.

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Open Settings › Sync on each device and choose the same folder in iCloud Drive (for example a folder called "Cryptum"). Every device keeps its own record of changes in that folder, so edits from different devices don't overwrite each other. Make sure iCloud Drive is turned on for each device in its system Settings.

My changes aren't showing up on another device.

Check that both devices chose the same sync folder (Settings › Sync shows the folder and when it last synced), then tap Sync Now. iCloud Drive can take a minute to move files, especially on mobile data or in Low Power Mode.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Try "Add a task in Cryptum" or "What's on my Today in Cryptum". Every action is also in the Shortcuts app.

How do I back up my data?

Settings › Data & Backup › Export Backup saves everything as a single file you can keep anywhere.

Does Cryptum collect my data?

No. There's no account and no tracking, and your data never reaches the developer. See the Privacy Policy.
