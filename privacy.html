Cryptum Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version: Cryptum doesn't collect any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your tasks, projects and thoughts stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud Drive. The developer never sees them.

What Cryptum stores, and where

Everything you add to Cryptum (tasks, Projects, Jobs, notes, checklists, thoughts, tags, pictures and links) is saved on the device you're using, inside the app's own storage.

Sync

Sync is optional. If you turn it on, you pick a folder in your own iCloud Drive, and each of your devices writes its changes there. That folder belongs to your iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it.

When your Mac and iPhone are on the same network, Cryptum can also pass changes directly between them so they arrive faster. That traffic goes only between your own devices, which must be paired with each other first.

Permissions Cryptum may ask for

  • Notifications, to remind you about tasks at the time you set. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Calendars (optional), to show your events in Today and Upcoming, and, if you choose, to put timed tasks on a "Cryptum" calendar. Calendar data stays on your device and in your own calendar accounts.
  • Siri and Shortcuts, so you can add and check tasks by voice. Siri requests are handled by Apple.
  • Local network, for the direct Mac ↔ iPhone link described above.

Web links

If you add a web link to a thought, Cryptum fetches that page's title and preview image directly from the website, the same way a browser would. Nothing else is sent.

Information collected

None. Cryptum doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services, doesn't show ads, and doesn't share or sell anything, because it has nothing to share. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports along under its own terms.

Children

Cryptum doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on that device. If you use sync, you can also delete the sync folder from iCloud Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
